LifeCue is a private on-device reminder app. There is no login.

This version adds LifeCue Pro Lifetime, a non-consumable in-app purchase.

Product ID: com.lifecue.app.pro.lifetime
Type: Non-Consumable

Free: tap + and Add Reminder. Home, Calendar, People, and Contexts stay available. Snooze, complete, and edit stay free.

Pro: Upload Image, Take Photo, repeating reminders, Forward, and Backup and Restore.

How to review IAP:
1. Allow Notifications and Camera when prompted.
2. Tap + then Upload Image or Take Photo. These open the Pro paywall.
3. Or create a manual reminder and choose a repeating schedule. That opens the paywall.
4. Or open a reminder and choose Forward.
5. Or open More then Backup and Restore.
6. Settings also has Unlock Pro. Restore Purchases is on the paywall.
7. Use a sandbox Apple ID. No app account or login is required.

Image extraction creates a draft only. No notification is scheduled until the user confirms.
